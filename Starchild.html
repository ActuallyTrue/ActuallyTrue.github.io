Click here to play Starchild.

StarChild was made for the 2018 Game Maker's Toolkit 2019 Game Jam. The theme of the jam was only one, which is why you only have one comet to attack and defend with. I worked on this game as the lead game designer and a general programmer on a team of 5 people. I implemented the camera movement, the enemy aiming and shooting, and the enemy spawning system which prioritizes spawning enemies offscreen. I also implemented the camera movement and all animations seen in the game.
