Click here to download Project Chronograph.

Project Chronograph was my first foray into game development. I was the lead programmer on the team. The game originally started as a metroidvania, but it didn't work out so it became a puzzle platformer. We had some troubles with art at the end which is why there are floating blocks as the cores the player has to possess.

Keyboard:
WASD or arrow keys to move, Spacebar to jump, K to dash, O to speed up when possessing, i to slow down when possessing
PS4 Controller:
Control stick or Dpad to move, Square to dash, X to jump, R2 to speed up when possessing, L2 to slow down when possessing
Gameplay:
The objective is to get to the end of the level. To do this, you can dash into the cores (gray blocks) to possess them. When you jump out of them, you'll recieve any momentum the core has plus your jump. Try to speed up and slow down the cores to get the perfect jumps needed to clear the level!
